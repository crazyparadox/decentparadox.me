Folio · Privacy Policy · Updated 5 October 2026

Privacy Policy

Folio is a reading app for technical books and courses. This page explains what it does with your information. The short version: Folio doesn't collect your personal data, has no accounts, and has no advertising or analytics.

What stays on your device

Your reading position, quiz results, highlights, notes, saved AI answers and reading preferences are stored only on your device. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

What we collect

Nothing. Folio has no sign-in, no tracking, no third-party analytics SDKs and no advertising. We don't know who you are or what you read.

Ask AI (optional)

Ask AI is off unless you turn it on. If you add your own Claude API key in Settings, then when you choose "Ask AI" on a selected passage, Folio sends that passage, the text of the chapter you are reading and your question to Anthropic's API (api.anthropic.com) so it can answer. This is a direct connection from your device to Anthropic using your key; it does not pass through any server of ours. Anthropic's handling of that data is described in their privacy policy at anthropic.com/privacy. Your key is stored in your device's Keychain.

If you don't add a key, Folio uses Apple's on-device model where your device supports it. In that case nothing leaves your device.

Videos and links

Some lessons include a companion YouTube video. When you play one, YouTube (Google) serves it under its own privacy policy. Links inside books open in your browser and are subject to those sites' policies.

Children

Folio is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.
